session: either (a) a different, correctly-stated fact from elsewhere in
the same KOST Function 7.10 course, repurposed here as a wrong answer to
*this* question (a "swapped-fact" or "wrong-checkpoint" distractor), or

around passenger baggage or is otherwise not a dedicated freight
